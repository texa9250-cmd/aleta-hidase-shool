አለታ-ህዳሴ የትምህርት ቤት ታሪክ የተማሪዎች፣ የመምህራን እና የማኅበረሰቡ የጋራ ታሪክ ነው። በየትውልዱ ተማሪዎች እውቀትን እየተማሩ፣ ሕልማቸውን እያሳደጉ እና ለወደፊቱ እየተዘጋጁ በዚህ ታሪክ ላይ የራሳቸውን አሻራ አኑረዋል።

For generations, አለታ-ህዳሴ Secondary and Preparatory School has been a place where young people come to learn, discover their strengths, and prepare for what comes next. Students, educators, and families have each added something lasting to the life of the school.

Every new class begins another chapter: built on curiosity, strengthened by community, and carried forward with hope.
